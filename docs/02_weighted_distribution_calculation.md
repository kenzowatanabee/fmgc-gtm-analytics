# 02. How to Calculate Weighted Distribution (WD / DP)

Weighted Distribution measures the **market relevance** of the store outlets carrying your product, rather than just counting door numbers.

---

## 1. The Mathematical Formula

$$\text{WD (\%)} = \left( \frac{\text{Total Category Sales in Stores Carrying Your Product}}{\text{Total Category Sales in ALL Stores of the Market}} \right) \times 100$$

> **Crucial Rule:** The numerator uses **Total Category Sales** in your target outlets, NOT just your own product's sales revenue.

---

## 2. Step-by-Step Practical Example

Consider a regional market with **5 stores** selling toothpaste. Total category turnover for toothpaste in this market is **$100,000/month**.

| Outlet | Category Turnover (Toothpaste) | Your Product Present? |
| :--- | :---: | :---: |
| **Store A** (Hypermarket) | $50,000 | **Yes** |
| **Store B** (Medium Supermarket) | $30,000 | **Yes** |
| **Store C** (Neighborhood Store) | $10,000 | **No** |
| **Store D** (Local Grocery) | $6,000 | **No** |
| **Store E** (Convenience Store) | $4,000 | **No** |
| **TOTAL MARKET** | **$100,000** | — |

### Step A: Numeric Distribution (ND)
$$\text{ND} = \frac{2 \text{ Stores}}{5 \text{ Stores}} \times 100 = \mathbf{40\%}$$

### Step B: Weighted Distribution (WD)
1. Add Category Potential for active stores (Store A + Store B):
   $$\$50,000 + \$30,000 = \$80,000$$
2. Apply the WD formula:
   $$\text{WD} = \left( \frac{\$80,000}{\$100,000} \right) \times 100 = \mathbf{80\%}$$

---

## 3. Interpreting ND vs. WD

```
High WD (80%) + Low ND (40%)  ==> Ideal strategy (High-volume Key Accounts captured)
High ND (80%) + Low WD (40%)  ==> Inefficient dispersion (Lacking Key Account listings)
```
