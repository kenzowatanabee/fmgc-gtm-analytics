# 01. Key Distribution Metrics & FMCG Glossary

In Fast-Moving Consumer Goods (FMCG), trade distribution metrics evaluate physical accessibility, sales potential, and logistics efficiency.

---

## 1. Distribution Metrics

### Numeric Distribution (ND / DN)
* **Definition:** Percentage of target store outlets in a region that stock a given SKU or brand.
* **Formula:**
  $$\text{ND (\%)} = \left( \frac{\text{Number of Stores Carrying Product}}{\text{Total Stores Selling Category}} \right) \times 100$$
* **Focus:** Physical availability and reach.

### Weighted Distribution (WD / DP)
* **Definition:** Percentage of total category sales volume represented by the stores in which the product is present.
* **Formula:**
  $$\text{WD (\%)} = \left( \frac{\text{Total Category Sales in Stores Carrying Product}}{\text{Total Category Sales in ALL Stores}} \right) \times 100$$
* **Focus:** Commercial potential and revenue concentration.

---

## 2. In-Store Execution Metrics

| KPI | Full Name | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **OOS** | Out of Stock | Percentage of stores or shelf space where the SKU is unavailable. |
| **SoS** | Share of Shelf | Percentage of total category facing/shelf space occupied by the brand. |
| **Sales Velocity** | Sales per Store per Week | Rate at which a product sells once placed on the shelf (Sell-Out speed). |

---

## 3. Commercial Sales Flow Stages

1. **Primary Sales:** Industry sales to Distributors or Wholesalers.
2. **Secondary Sales:** Distributor/Wholesaler sales to Retailers.
3. **Tertiary Sales (Sell-Out):** Retailer sales directly to the end Consumer.

---

## 4. Supply Chain & Service Level KPIs

* **OTIF (On-Time In-Full):** Percentage of orders delivered within the agreed delivery window and complete as ordered.
* **Order Cycle Time:** Lead time between customer order confirmation and physical warehouse delivery.
* **Logistics Cost % Sales:** Total warehousing and freight expenses as a percentage of net turnover.
